# [DataRealm Humanoid Application Lab](https://humanoidapplab.com "DataRealm Humanoid Application Lab")

New robots. Real-world work. We’re building the software that connects humanoids to the industrial world.

---

### :robot: About Us

At **DataRealm Humanoid Application Lab** (`HumanoidAppLab`), we bridge the gap between advanced humanoid robotics and real-world industrial environments. Our software systems enable robust perception, dexterous manipulation, fleet orchestration, and mission-critical autonomy on factory floors, logistics centers, and complex operating environments.

---

### :dart: Core Focus Areas

- **Industrial Integration & Middleware**: Seamless connectivity between humanoid platforms, PLC systems, warehouse management systems (WMS), and SCADA.
- **Teleoperation & Supervisory Control**: High-fidelity, low-latency control systems for remote teleoperation and shared human-robot autonomy.
- **Manipulation & Dexterous Skills**: Task learning, trajectory planning, and tactile grasping tailored for industrial workflows.
- **Safety & Compliance**: Deterministic fail-safes, safety zone monitoring, and industrial safety compliance frameworks.
